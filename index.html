How it works

No order book and no oracle you have to trust. Positions are SOL in the vault with a memo you can read on Solscan; prices are the last trade on each coin's deepest pool.

01

Pick two coins

One you think wins, one you think loses. The ratio between them is your chart.

Rules

  • Marks are every 5 minutes on the clock (:00, :05, :10…). Opens and closes take effect at the next mark.
  • Price = last trade before the mark on each coin's deepest pool, from 1-minute candles. Ratio = long coin ÷ short coin.
  • At each mark, × the ratio move × the matched size crosses from the losing side to the winning side, split by equity.
  • Matched size = the smaller of the two sides. Size beyond it waits and earns nothing until someone takes the other side.
  • Equity under 1% of your stake closes the position. Coins that can't be priced at your first mark get a full refund.
  • Open = SOL to the vault with memo SPREAD|OPEN|long|short. Close = memo SPREAD|CLOSE|tx from the same wallet. Vault:
